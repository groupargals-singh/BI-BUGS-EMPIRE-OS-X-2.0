# Master Constitution — Supporting Entry Point

**Status:** Supporting summary; review against the canonical constitution before adoption.

Canonical sources: `../MASTER_SPECIFICATION/02_ENGINEERING_CONSTITUTION.md` and `../MASTER_SPECIFICATION/06_ENGINEERING_RULES.md`.

## Governing principles
- User and business authority remain explicit; high-impact or irreversible actions require authorized approval.
- Security, privacy, data integrity, and auditability take precedence over convenience.
- Separate proposal, design, implementation, verification, and release status.
- Do not claim a capability is live without reproducible evidence.
- Preserve history and document exceptions, ownership, and rollback conditions.

This supporting page does not replace the canonical constitution or prove enforcement in code.
